A. Grouped by document type

Sections collapse like folders. Good when the queue is huge and one type dominates. Costs an extra tap to reach urgent items of a small type.

B. One list, urgency first, filter chips

Everything sorted by priority then age. Colour bar = priority. Chips narrow by area. Tap a card to review, then decide on the detail screen. Long-press to select several for bulk approve.

C. Focus queue, one at a time

Full-screen card with the facts and big Approve / Reject buttons, then the next one appears. Fastest for clearing, but no overview, and harder to find one specific document.

My lean: B as the inbox, with the one-at-a-time view (C) opened from it as "Review next" once you are inside a detail. A is the fallback if one type (Journal Entry) swamps everything else.
