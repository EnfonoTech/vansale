Part A — The driver app

What the app is for

One Android app for selling off a van: invoice a customer at their shop, take the cash, print the receipt, handle returns, and keep going when there is no signal. Everything the driver books lands in ERPNext as a real Sales Invoice, Payment Entry or Stock Entry — there is no separate ledger to reconcile.

Two things about the app are worth knowing before you start. It is offline-first: work booked without signal is queued and sent later, so a dead zone never stops a sale. And it is scoped to one van: the driver only sees their own customers, their own van's stock and their own documents.

A1First run — the server address

The first screen asks for your company's server address. This is entered once per install and kept until the app is uninstalled.

  • Type the host only — van.company.com. https:// is added for you.
  • If you paste a full browser URL, everything after the host is ignored.
  • The address is tested before it is saved, so a typo is caught here rather than failing silently on every screen afterwards.
If it will not connect

"Could not reach that address" means the spelling or the signal — check both. "Not a Van Sale server" means you reached a real server that does not run this app; you have the wrong address, not a broken phone.

A2Signing in, and the PIN

Sign in once with the ERPNext email and password the office gave you. After that the app stays signed in until it is uninstalled — the password is not asked for again.

If PIN unlock is switched on for you, you choose a 4-digit PIN on first sign-in and re-enter it after 2 hours idle. That is a quick unlock, not a new login. Change it any time from More → Change PIN.

Forgot the PIN

Nobody can read it back to you — call the office and ask them to reset it (Part B11). Your queued offline work survives a PIN reset.

A4Making a sale

Home → New invoice, or Sales → +.

On the formWhat to know
Customer Tap to open a search sheet and type any part of the name or code. Only your own customers are listed. New creates one on the spot.
Catalog Only items with stock in your van appear. An empty catalog means the van is empty, not that the app is broken — the message tells you which warehouse to ask about.
Qty / Rate Rate is pre-filled from the price list for this customer. You may override it if the office allows.
UOM If enabled, switch a line between that item's own units (Pcs / Box / Carton) and the rate re-fetches for the new unit. Only that item's units are offered — never the whole UOM list.
Discount Per line, or one invoice-level amount. The invoice-level amount is split across lines proportionally and the split is previewed before you save.
Cash / Credit Cash records the payment with the invoice. Credit leaves it outstanding for collection later.

Save as draft keeps it editable and posts nothing. Submit is final: stock leaves the van and the ledger is written. A submitted invoice cannot be edited — correct it with a return.

About the tax figure

The tax shown while you type is a live preview using this customer's real tax rate — a zero-rated customer shows no tax at all. If your price list is tax-inclusive, the line rate you type already contains the tax and the totals show incl. next to the tax line. The final breakup is always recomputed by the server on save.

A5Printing

Open a submitted invoice and tap Print. The app fetches the document and hands it to Android's own print dialog, so you can pick any printer you have paired, or save a PDF.

No browser login needed

Printing does not open a browser and does not ask you to sign in to ERPNext. If you are ever asked to log in to print, you are on an old build.

A6Collecting payment

Home → Collect payment. Pick the customer, enter the amount, choose the mode of payment. The app shows what they owe and applies the money against the oldest invoices first.

Taking money without applying it to an invoice leaves it unapplied — the office sees that separately in the Collection Summary report, because it is the usual reason a customer's statement disagrees with what the driver says they collected.

A7Returns

Open the original invoice → Return. Tick the lines coming back and set the quantity; you cannot return more than was sold.

A reason is required. Pick one of Damaged Expired Wrong Item Customer Refused Short Delivery Other and add detail in the note box. Submit stays disabled until a reason is chosen.

This is not paperwork for its own sake: the office reports returns by reason, which is how a bad batch or a mis-picked route gets spotted.

A9Working offline

Keep selling. Invoices, payments, returns, customers and stock moves are queued on the phone and sent when signal returns. The sync badge in the header shows how many items are waiting.

Some queued items cannot be sent, and the app is explicit about whose problem it is:

ChipMeaningWhat you do
Retrying No signal yet. Nothing. It goes on its own.
Needs edit Something in what you entered is wrong or missing. Tap Edit, fix it, retry.
Needs admin A data problem in ERPNext — typically an item with no valuation, or no stock in the van. Editing cannot fix it. Call the office. Once they fix it, tap Retry.
Not permitted Your user lacks a permission. Call the office.
Never uninstall with items pending

Queued work lives on the phone. Uninstalling, clearing app data, or switching the app to another server discards anything not yet sent. Check the sync badge is clear first.

B2Vansale Settings — the global switches

/app/vansale-settings. Three master switches for the whole site. Every one can be overridden per van, and PIN can be overridden per user.

SettingFieldDefaultEffect when off
Enable route planning enable_route on Route disappears from the app. The fourth tab and the fourth home card become Van Stock. Route screens are unreachable even by deep link.
Require PIN unlock require_pin on No PIN is set or asked for. Drivers go straight to Home after login, and Change PIN is hidden.
Allow UOM change on invoice lines allow_uom_change on Every line sells in the item's stock UOM. The unit selector is hidden.
When changes reach the phone

On the driver's next app start, or when they switch back to the app from the background, or when signal returns. No re-login needed. On builds older than v1.0.29 the driver must log out and back in.

B4Assigned Users — who drives this van

The Assigned Users table is what connects a person to a van. Adding a row here does the work for you on save: it creates the Company, Branch, Warehouse and Cost Center user permissions and grants the role. Removing a row takes them away again.

ColumnNameNotes
UseruserThe ERPNext user who signs in on the phone.
RoleroleVan User for a driver, Van Manager for a supervisor. Granted automatically.
Sales Personsales_personTagged onto every invoice for commission, and it is how the driver's customer list is scoped.
PIN unlockpin_modePer-driver override. The most specific setting in the whole system.
One van per user

A user belongs to one van. Listing the same person on two vans makes their van attribution ambiguous — reports resolve the van from the person who created the document, so a second row makes the numbers unreliable.

B5Warehouse and cost centre

The first row is the default. The first warehouse becomes the driver's van warehouse — the one their catalog is filtered by and stock is sold out of. Add more rows only if the van genuinely draws from more than one. Same rule for cost centres.

This is the single most common setup mistake

Rows in the wrong order means the driver sells from the main store instead of the van, and van stock reports show nothing. Check the order, not just the contents.

B6Pricing — which price list wins

First match wins, top to bottom:

→

→
Customer Groupdefault_price_list

Selling Settingssite default

The van sits above Customer Group on purpose: a van running a discounted route should not fall back to the group's sticker price when it reaches a customer nobody has classified.

Rates are fetched per item and unit, so a Box price and a Pcs price are both honoured. With no Item Price for the chosen unit, the stock-unit price is multiplied by the conversion factor.

B7Tax

The rate is resolved the same way the desk form does it — the customer's Tax Category, through Tax Rule, to a Sales Taxes and Charges Template, falling back to the company default. Nothing about tax is configured in Van Sale itself.

Customertax_category
→
Tax Rulematch
→
Templaterate + inclusive
→
fallbackcompany default

So a zero-rated or export customer is taxed correctly in the van, with no van setting involved: give the customer the right Tax Category and the app follows.

Inclusive price lists

If a template's rows are marked Included in Print Rate, the price already contains the tax. The app then backs the tax out of the rate the driver typed instead of adding it on top, and the totals show incl. with a Net of tax line.

Check this before going live

Inclusive versus exclusive changes what the customer is charged. Post one test invoice for a normal customer and one for a zero-rated customer, and compare the app's totals against the saved document, before any real selling. A wrong template overcharges by the full tax on every line.

Compound templates — anything using On Previous Row — cannot be collapsed into one percentage. The app says the preview is approximate and shows the server's exact figure after save.

B8Document numbering per van

Set Document Prefix on the van, e.g. VAN1-, and save. The app then numbers that van's documents independently:

DocumentSeriesUsed for
Sales InvoiceVAN1-INV-.YYYY.-.####Normal sales
Sales InvoiceVAN1-CN-.YYYY.-.####Credit notes / returns
Payment EntryVAN1-PE-.YYYY.-.####Collections
Stock EntryVAN1-SE-.YYYY.-.####Van loading and transfers

The prefix is upper-cased and given a single trailing dash automatically — van1 becomes VAN1-. Leave it blank and the van uses the site's normal series.

Do not hand-edit the series rows

Each series carries a per-document abbreviation — INV, CN, PE, SE — and that is what keeps the counters separate. Frappe counts by the resolved prefix, so if two document types ever shared one template they would share one counter: invoice 41 followed by receipt 42. The table is read-only for that reason.

Returns get their own abbreviation so credit notes never consume invoice numbers. A supervisor with System, Accounts or Stock Manager can still pick a series by hand on the desk; the app does not override that.

B9Which setting wins

Most specific first. The first level that says Enabled or Disabled decides.

Assigned User rowpin_mode
→
Vansale Configurationroute · pin · uom
→
Vansale Settingsglobal

Follow Global means "don't decide here, ask the next level up". A van set to Enabled keeps its module even when the global switch is off — that is how you run route planning on one van and not the rest.

Only PIN has the per-user level. Route and UOM resolve van, then global.

B10Reports and the dashboard

ReportAnswers
Van Daily SettlementThe evening cash check. Per van per day: invoices, returns, gross → net sales, cash taken at the point of sale, what went on credit, outstanding.
Van Collection SummaryReceipts by van and mode of payment, splitting applied from unapplied.
Van Stock PositionWhat is on board each van now, flagging items that have stock but no valuation rate.
Van Visit CompliancePlanned vs visited vs skipped, coverage %, and how many stops produced an invoice.

The Van Sales dashboard carries four number cards — sales, invoices, collection, outstanding — and three charts: daily sales trend, sales by warehouse, collection by mode.

How a document is attributed to a van

By who created it, matched to the Assigned Users table. Payment Entries carry no warehouse or sales person, so the creator is the only link that works across invoices, payments and visits. A document the office creates on a driver's behalf therefore shows a blank Van. Van Stock Position is the exception — it attributes by warehouse, so it stays correct when the office loads the van.

B11Everyday admin tasks

Reset a driver's PIN

Open the van → Actions → Reset Driver PIN → pick the driver. Their next login asks them to set a new one. Queued offline work is untouched. A driver cannot reset their own this way — they use Change PIN, which requires the current one.

Clear a “Needs admin” sync error

Almost always an item with stock but no valuation rate, or no stock in the van. Run Van Stock Position and look at the No Valuation column. Fix it by posting the stock into the van warehouse properly, or by setting a valuation rate on the item. Then tell the driver to hit Retry.

Do not reach for Allow Zero Valuation Rate

It makes the error disappear by booking zero cost of goods, which quietly destroys margin reporting. Post the stock instead.

Load a van

A Material Transfer Stock Entry from the main store to the van warehouse. Until stock is in that warehouse the driver's catalog is empty by design — the app will not let them sell what the van does not carry.

Take a van out of service

Remove the driver's row from Assigned Users; their permissions and role are withdrawn on save. Keep the van record for history.

B13Setting up a new van — checklist

  1. Create the Warehouse for the van, and a Cost Center if you track per van.
  2. Create the ERPNext User for the driver.
  3. Create a Sales Person for the driver.
  4. Create the Vansale Configuration: van code, company, first warehouse row, first cost centre row.
  5. Add the driver to Assigned Users with role Van User and their Sales Person. Save — permissions and role are applied for you.
  6. Set module overrides if this van differs from the global switches.
  7. Set Document Prefix if the van needs its own numbering, and save.
  8. Set Selling Price List if the van has its own pricing tier.
  9. Confirm the driver's customers carry their Sales Person in Sales Team.
  10. Confirm customers have a Tax Category where it matters.
  11. Load the van with a Material Transfer Stock Entry.
  12. Install the APK, enter the server address, sign in, set a PIN.
  13. Post one test invoice and one test return end-to-end, then check Van Daily Settlement.
